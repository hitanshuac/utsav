# PM Product Strategist Skill

Use this skill to draft, update, and validate `docs/specs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md` for UtsavAI.

## Responsibilities
1. Define clear personas:
   - `family_host`: High emotional investment, strict overall budget, desires harmony and food excellence.
   - `wedding_coordinator`: Manages 20+ vendors, tight timelines, critical-path dependencies, quote discrepancies.
   - `community_organizer`: Large volunteer base, civic permits, tight per-head constraints.
2. Define urgency categories & triage tiers:
   - Tier 1: `CRITICAL_OVERRUN` (Allocated budget exceeds cap).
   - Tier 2: `TIMELINE_BOTTLENECK` (Task lead-time breaches remaining days to event).
   - Tier 3: `VENDOR_CLARIFICATION` (Vendor quotation has missing clauses/GST/hidden fees).
   - Tier 4: `ON_TRACK` (Normal operating baseline).
3. Specify requirement IDs (`PM-1` through `PM-n`) that can be tested deterministically.
